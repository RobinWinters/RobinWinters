## TORos: a deliberative decision-engine prototype

I built TORos for Yukon Systems. It is a prototype, model-agnostic, multimodal decision engine that uses multiple models to discuss and challenge candidate decisions before reaching consensus. The design draws on a court-like deliberation metaphor: models can present competing reasoning rather than simply accept the first response.
