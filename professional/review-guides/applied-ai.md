The <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/part-one-orchestration-alignment-through-agentic-consensus-lh2tf/">Yukon company article dated May 5, 2025</a> describes the prototype and includes an early CLI screenshot. My <a href="https://github.com/RobinWinters/RobinWinters/blob/Radpository/work/yukon-systems.md">work account</a> separates that published description from my first-person contribution statement.</p>
<p>The technical discussion is about how to organize disagreement and reach a decision. Agreement alone does not establish correctness. Useful review questions include how conflicting model outputs are represented, what should cause abstention, how a decision can be explained and which failure modes require human review. Public source, reproducible benchmarks and customer deployment are not established here.</p>
